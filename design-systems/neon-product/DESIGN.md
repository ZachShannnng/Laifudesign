# Neon Product

> Category: Technical Product
> Crisp dark surfaces, cyan emphasis, and precise product storytelling.

## Color

- **Primary:** `#06B6D4`
- **Secondary:** `#A3E635`
- **Background:** `#0B1020`
- **Surface:** `#111827`
- **Text:** `#E5E7EB`
- **Muted:** `#94A3B8`
- **Border:** `#263244`

## Typography

- Use Inter or system sans-serif.
- Code-like labels may use JetBrains Mono.
- Keep letter spacing at 0.

## Layout

- Product UI or workflow evidence should carry the hero.
- Avoid decorative gradient blobs.
- Maintain strong contrast and explicit focus states.
