# Quiet Enterprise

> Category: Enterprise Productivity
> Restrained, practical interface language for repeated operational work.

## Color

- **Primary:** `#2563EB`
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Background:** `#F6F7F9`
- **Text:** `#111827`
- **Muted:** `#6B7280`
- **Border:** `#D9DEE7`
- **Success:** `#15803D`
- **Warning:** `#B45309`
- **Danger:** `#B91C1C`

## Typography

- Font family: Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui
- Use 12/14/16/20/24/30 scale.
- Keep dashboard headings compact and functional.

## Layout

- Prefer side navigation, top filter bars, dense tables, and clear empty states.
- Cards are for repeated modules only; do not wrap whole pages in cards.
- Border radius: 6px for controls and cards.

## Interaction

- All actions need hover/focus/disabled states.
- Destructive actions require visual distinction and confirmation affordance.
