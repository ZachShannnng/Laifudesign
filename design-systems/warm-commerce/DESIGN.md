# Warm Commerce

> Category: Commerce & Lifestyle
> Warm, trustworthy, conversion-oriented without feeling loud.

## Color

- **Primary:** `#C2410C`
- **Secondary:** `#0F766E`
- **Background:** `#FFF7ED`
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text:** `#1C1917`
- **Muted:** `#78716C`
- **Border:** `#FED7AA`

## Typography

- Use 14/16/18/24/32/40 scale.
- Product names and prices must be easy to scan.
- CTA text should be concrete and action-oriented.

## Layout

- Use clear product grids, benefit strips, and trust signals.
- Keep checkout or reservation flows calm and predictable.
